## 1. Nature of the service

PAN issues unique digital identities for product units and cartons under a named producer’s workspace and allows consumers to check whether a scanned or entered code matches an active identity in that workspace.

**A successful PAN result means only that the code matches an active digital identity issued under the named producer’s PAN workspace.** It does **not**:

- prove that the physical item was manufactured by that producer;
- guarantee product contents, quality, safety, efficacy or packaging integrity;
- constitute NAFDAC registration, SON certification, or any other regulatory approval or endorsement;
- replace independent due diligence by the consumer or buyer.

Regulatory numbers entered by a producer are displayed as producer-provided information unless and until PAN has a documented, authorised validation integration with the relevant authority.
